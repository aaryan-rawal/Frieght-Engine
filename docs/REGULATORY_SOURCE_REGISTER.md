## Fail-closed evidence

The regulatory evaluator requires a configured trusted source, policy version, assessment/expiry timestamps and a sourced decision for every required check. The trusted-source allow-list is empty by default. Missing, stale, incomplete or untrusted evidence yields `unknown`, which cannot authorize a candidate. Existing tests cover the missing-assessment and untrusted-source cases.

Do not populate the trusted-source allow-list, claim a regulatory pass, or make a legal compliance claim based solely on this register. A release requires current legal review, owner-approved policy version, provider credentials/agreements where needed, integration tests and effective-date/exemption tests.
